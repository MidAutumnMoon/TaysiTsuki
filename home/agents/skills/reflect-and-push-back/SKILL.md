I can't hold the entire codebase in my head, and working from my instructions narrows your view to the same spots. Even when our plan or implementation looks fine up close, there may be parts of the system and cases neither of us checked. Explore beyond the files in focus and look for what we missed.

Missed details might introduce subtle issues, or make our approach fight the rest of the system. If that's the case, don't propose band-aids just to keep our current framing intact. *Think outside the box.* Don't introduce regressions, but policies and assumptions can be loosened.

Don't edit code in this pass. Show me concrete findings where our thinking has gaps, or tell me plainly if it holds up.
